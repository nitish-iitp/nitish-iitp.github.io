Nitish Kumar

Ph.D. Scholar, Computer Science & Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Patna

I am a doctoral researcher at IIT Patna working at the intersection of large language models, information retrieval and multimodal reasoning, with a focus on sports. My work looks at how to make AI-generated insights trustworthy: ranking LLM outputs with hallucination awareness, verifying factual claims against structured databases, localizing key moments in long broadcasts, and supporting tactical decisions with causal, outcome-free representations.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence at NIT Agartala, served as an Assistant Professor at GMR Institute of Technology, and worked as Research Staff at the Technology Innovation Hub, IIT Patna.
